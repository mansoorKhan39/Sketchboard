# Sketchboard – User Manual

Nov 17, 2026 · @Mansoor

Sketchboard is a free online whiteboard where you can draw, add sticky notes and connect ideas, alone or with other people at the same time.

## 1. Welcome

**What you can do**

- Put sticky notes, shapes, text and pen drawings on an endless canvas.
- Join objects with connector lines that follow them when they move.
- Start from a template: Blank board, Brainstorm, Kanban or Flowchart.
- Share a link so others can view or edit, and see their cursors live.
- Use light or dark mode.

**Two ways to use it**

|  | Guest (no account) | With an account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where boards are saved | In this browser only | In your account, on any device |
| Share a board | No | Yes |
| Work together live | No | Yes |
| Cost | Free | Free |

A guest board stays on the computer and browser where you made it. Clearing the browser's site data deletes it, so create an account for boards you want to keep.

**What you need:** a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox and an internet connection. A mouse is easiest; a trackpad works too.

## 2. Getting started

**Create an account (recommended)**

1. Open the Sketchboard address you were given.
2. Choose **Create an account** (the sign-in page has a link to it).
3. Enter your name, e-mail and a password, then press **Create account**. You are signed in straight away.
4. Next time, press **Sign in** with the same e-mail and password.

If the e-mail is already used, you will see a message; sign in instead.

**Your first board**

1. On the start page, under **Start a board**, pick a template, or press **New board** for an empty one.
2. The board opens. Click the name at the top (it starts as "Untitled board") and type a name.
3. Pick a tool on the left, or press its key, then click on the canvas.
4. Your work saves by itself. The top bar shows the save state.

**Already made boards as a guest?** After you sign in, the start page offers **Move to my account**. Press it to bring your browser boards into your account.

**Sign out** from the account menu: the round button with your initial at the top right. When you are not signed in, that spot shows a Sign in button instead.

## 3. The board screen

**Left: the toolbar.** Click a tool or press its key.

| Tool | Key | Use it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Select | V | Click, drag, resize and rotate objects |
| Sticky note | N | Place a coloured note and type on it |
| Shape | S | Draw a rectangle, ellipse or diamond |
| Pen | P | Draw freehand |
| Connector | L | Join two objects with a line |
| Text | T | Place plain text |

**Top: the board bar.** The arrow button (tooltip "All boards") returns to the start page. Next to it is the board name; click it to rename. The right side shows the save state, the people on the board, the Share button, the theme switch and your account button.

**Save state** (top bar)

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| Saving… | Your latest change is being stored |
| Saved | Everything is stored |
| Connecting… | Opening the live link for a signed-in board |
| Live | Live collaboration is on; changes are shared as you make them |
| Reconnecting… your changes are kept | The link dropped; your edits are kept and sent again when it returns |
| View only | You opened a link that lets you look but not change |

**Floating bar: properties.** When you select something, a small bar appears under the top bar. It shows what you selected and the options for it: colour swatches for notes, shapes, pen strokes, text and connectors; Rect / Ellipse / Diamond for shapes; Straight / Elbow and an Arrow switch for connectors; and **Duplicate** and **Delete**.

**Bottom: view and history.** Undo and Redo, Zoom out and Zoom in, the zoom percentage (click it to reset to 100%), and a fit button that shows everything on the board.

**Empty board.** A card says "This board is empty" and lists the tool keys. It does not block clicks, so you can start drawing straight away.

## 4. Drawing and editing

**Add things**

- **Sticky note:** press N, click the canvas, type. Pick another colour in the floating bar.
- **Shape:** press S and drag on the canvas. Choose Rect, Ellipse or Diamond and a fill colour in the floating bar.
- **Pen:** press P and drag to draw. Pick a colour in the floating bar.
- **Text:** press T, click, and type.
- **Connector:** press L, then click the first object and the second object. The line stays attached when you move either one. In the floating bar choose Straight or Elbow, switch the Arrow on or off, and pick a colour.

**Change things (Select tool, key V)**

- Click an object to select it; drag it to move it.
- Drag the corner handles to resize and the round handle to rotate.
- Drag a box over empty canvas to select several objects. Moving one moves all of them together.
- **Double-click** a sticky note, shape or text to edit its words. Click outside, or press Esc, to finish.
- **Duplicate** and **Delete** are in the floating bar; Delete also works with the Delete key.
- Copy and paste with Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V; duplicate with Ctrl+D.

**Undo and redo.** Ctrl+Z undoes your last action and Ctrl+Shift+Z (or Ctrl+Y) redoes it. On a shared board, undo only reverses what you did, never what other people did. Creating a sticky note and typing its text count as two steps.

**On a Mac,** use the Cmd key wherever this manual says Ctrl.

## 5. Moving around and shortcuts

**Move around the board**

- **Zoom:** scroll the mouse wheel; the point under the pointer stays where it is. On a trackpad, pinch or hold Ctrl and scroll. Zoom goes from 10% to 400%.
- **Pan:** hold Space and drag.
- **Show everything:** press Shift+1, or use the fit button at the bottom.
- **Back to 100%:** click the zoom percentage.

**Keyboard shortcuts**

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| V | Select tool |
| N | Sticky note |
| S | Shape |
| P | Pen |
| T | Text |
| L | Connector |
| Space + drag | Pan |
| Shift + 1 | Fit everything on screen |
| Ctrl + Z | Undo |
| Ctrl + Shift + Z, or Ctrl + Y | Redo |
| Ctrl + C, Ctrl + V | Copy, paste |
| Ctrl + D | Duplicate |
| Delete | Delete the selection |
| Esc | Finish editing, or clear the selection |

Shortcuts do not fire while you are typing in a text box.

## 6. Your boards

**The start page**

- **Start a board:** pick a template to open a new board with ready content: *Blank board*, *Brainstorm*, *Kanban* or *Flowchart*.
- **Your boards:** every board you have, with a small preview. Click a board to open it.
- **New board:** a new empty board.
- **Search boards:** type to filter by name.
- The line under "Start a board" tells you where boards are saved: "Boards are saved to your account." when signed in, or "Boards are saved in this browser." for guests.

**Manage a board.** Press the options button on a board's card and choose:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Rename | Type a new name; an empty name becomes "Untitled board" |
| Duplicate | Makes a copy of the board |
| Delete | Press once, then press again where it says "Click again to delete". Deleting cannot be undone |

You can also rename a board from the name at the top of the board screen.

**Saving.** There is no Save button. Changes are saved a moment after you stop (about 1 second for an account, a third of a second for a guest board). Check the top bar for Saving… and Saved. If a message says a board could not be loaded or created, press **Try again**.

**Move guest boards to your account.** Sign in and press **Move to my account** on the start page. The boards are copied to your account and appear in your list.

## 7. Sharing and working together

Only boards in your account can be shared. A guest board shows "Sign in to share"; sign in and move the board to your account first.

**Share a board**

1. Open the board and press **Share** in the top bar.
2. Turn on **Anyone with the link**.
3. Choose **Can view** (they can look only) or **Can edit** (they can change the board).
4. Press **Copy** and send the link by chat or e-mail.

Anyone who opens the link can join, even without an account. Guests appear with the name "Guest"; signed-in people appear with their account name.

**While you work together**

- The top bar shows **Live** and a small avatar for each other person on the board.
- You see each person's pointer move across the canvas, with their name.
- Changes appear on every screen as they are made.
- Two people can change different things on the same object at once; if you both change the same property, the later change wins.
- People with **View only** see the board and cursors but have no drawing tools and cannot change anything.

**Change or stop sharing**

- Switch **Can view** and **Can edit** at any time.
- **Make a new link (the old one stops working)** if a link was passed to the wrong person.
- Turn **Anyone with the link** off to stop sharing. People who already have the board open keep their access until they reload the page.

**If you open a link that does not work,** you will see "This link is not active. Ask the owner for a new link."

## 8. Problems and questions

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| My guest boards disappeared | Guest boards live in one browser. Clearing site data, or using another browser or computer, hides them. Create an account to keep boards |
| Sign-up says the e-mail is already used | Press Sign in instead |
| The top bar never says Live | Live editing may be switched off or unreachable. The board still works alone and saves normally; reload later |
| It says "Reconnecting… your changes are kept" | Wait. Your edits are stored and sent again when the link returns |
| A message like "Slow down a little." | You sent changes too fast; ease off for a moment and the board catches up |
| I cannot draw or move anything | The top bar shows View only: the owner shared the board as Can view. Ask for a Can edit link |
| The share link says "This link is not active" | The owner turned sharing off or made a new link. Ask for the current link |
| Share button asks me to sign in | Only boards in an account can be shared |
| A board will not load | Press Try again, or reload the page |
| Undo did not remove what another person drew | By design: undo reverses only your own actions |
| Text on a dark board is hard to read | Switch the theme with the theme button; ink colours adapt in dark mode |

**Limits to know**

- A board holds up to 5,000 objects and about 3 MB.
- Zoom is between 10% and 400%.
- Stacking order (which object is on top) is not shared between people, so two people may see overlaps differently.
- Passwords cannot be reset yet and e-mails are not checked, so keep your password safe.
- Live editing on the free hosting can pause on a very busy day; boards keep saving normally.

**Privacy.** Your boards are visible only to you and to people who have a share link you turned on. Anyone holding a link can open the board, so share links only with people you trust.

**Help.** Contact the person who runs your copy of Sketchboard, and tell them what you were doing, what you expected and what you saw.

## Appendix: Update for Stages 6–11

### Add a picture

1. Choose the picture tool, or drag a PNG, JPG or WebP file onto the board.
2. The picture appears in the middle of your view. Drag it to move, use the corners to resize.

Messages you may see: "That file is not a picture.", "That picture is larger than 15 MB.", "Could not read that picture. Try a PNG, JPG or WebP file.", "That picture is too detailed to store. Try a smaller one."

### Export as PNG

Use Export in the top bar. If the board has nothing on it you see "This board is empty. Add something before exporting."

### Frames and Present

1. Press **F** and drag to draw a frame around part of the board.
2. Moving a frame moves everything whose centre is inside it.
3. Press **Present**. Frames show one by one in order. Use the arrow keys to step and Esc to leave.

### Comments

1. Press **C** and click where the comment belongs.
2. Type and send. Others can reply (up to 50 messages, 500 characters each) and anyone can resolve it.
3. Viewers see: "You can read comments, but not add them (view only)."

### Version history

1. Open **History** (shown to the board owner only).
2. Press Save version to keep the board as it is now. The last 5 saved versions are kept; "No versions yet." shows when there are none.
3. Press Restore. The button changes to "Replace board?"; press it again to confirm. Restore is a normal edit, so Undo brings your old board back.

Guests keep versions in their own browser only.

### Vote, react, timer

- **Vote:** choose the vote button on a sticky note. Each person has 5 votes. Press again to withdraw.
- **React:** pick an emoji in the bottom bar. It floats for about 3.5 seconds. You can react once every 0.4 seconds.
- **Timer:** start it from the bottom bar. Everyone sees it count down. It uses each computer's own clock, so small differences are normal.

### Mind map

Select a sticky note. **Tab** adds a child, **Enter** adds a sibling. The Add child button in the side panel does the same as Tab.

### Private mode

Turn it on to hide who wrote each sticky note. This only changes what is shown. It is not security, so do not rely on it for secrets.

### Templates

Nine templates are available, including five new ones. A template opens fitted to your screen.

### Shortcuts added

F frame, C comment, Tab add child, Enter add sibling.

### Troubleshooting

| Problem | Reason and fix |
| --- | --- |
| I cannot see History | Only the board owner sees it. Sign in as the owner. |
| I cannot comment or vote | You have view-only access. Ask the owner for edit access. |
| Picture will not add | It is too large or detailed. Use a smaller PNG, JPG or WebP. |
| A reaction stays on screen | The sender closed the tab before it faded. Refresh the page. |
